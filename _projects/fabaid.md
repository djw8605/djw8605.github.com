[FabAID](https://fabaid.io) is an NSF-funded project building a fabric of services to accelerate data-intensive and AI-driven science — federating storage, caching, discovery, and workflows into an open service layer.

My work focuses on cybersecurity and operations for the FabAID service fabric, helping keep distributed data services reliable and secure for research communities.

I contribute to operational practices that support scalable, trustworthy data delivery and researcher onboarding across the fabric.

I serve on the OSG executive team.
